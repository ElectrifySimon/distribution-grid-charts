Why the list is short

Six reports were downloaded and every figure in them was put through the reader: IEA Electricity Grids and Secure Energy Transitions, IEA World Energy Outlook 2026, Eurelectric Grids for Speed, ACER's 2024 infrastructure monitoring report, the EU DSO Entity annual reporting and IRENA's World Energy Transitions Outlook 2023. 285 chart panels were detected. A panel is published only when its legend labels appear verbatim in the page's text layer, no two series share a name, and the values pass an internal test such as shares summing to 100 or a category total matching the figure the report states in prose.

The rest failed for reasons worth naming: multi-panel composites share one legend across panels and the reader pairs it wrongly; several reports draw legends as images, leaving no text to verify against; and IRENA's flagship uses legends with repeated column headers that cannot be matched one to one. Those are reader limits, not publisher errors.
